**Status lifecycle:**
`⬜ planned` → `🚧 in progress (Claude)` → `📤 pending Replit` → `✅ done & deployed`

- **`📤 pending Replit`** is Replit's cue: _read that round's `Claude Handoffs/NNN-xxx.md`, run it, publish, verify, report._ Replit acts **only** on `📤` rounds.
- **Claude** flips a row to `🚧` when starting the round, and to `📤` (with the commit) when the handoff is pushed.
- **Replit** flips it to `✅ done & deployed` after verifying live.
